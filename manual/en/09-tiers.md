# 9. Free, Pro, and Cine

| Tier | Features |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Bluetooth photo transfer, Bluetooth remote (focus, shutter, video, burst, self-timer), USB camera gallery (manual pick & download originals), Phone photos gallery, star ratings, Photo editor (including .np3 style preview, edit RAW on phone, switch to lossless RAW), Photo location, QR code sharing (resized), Night mode |
| **Pro** | Everything in Free, plus: Wi‑Fi (One-tap connect, Wi‑Fi camera gallery), FTP receive, Auto receive (Wi‑Fi, USB), Remote shooting, Shooting aids, Shooting tools (Bulb, interval timer, exposure bracketing, focus shift), Remote self-timer, Ghost overlay, Bluetooth remote interval timer, Save JPEG only from RAW, Save compressed JPEG, Memory card backup, Delete from camera, Shooting without a card (phone only), Camera styles C1–C9, QR code sharing at full resolution & RAW, Panorama stitching, Shake (noise-reduction merge) |
| **Cine** | Everything in Pro, plus: Video monitor, LUTs, Anamorphic desqueeze, A/B rack focus, Ghost overlay in Video monitor |

Tapping any locked feature displays the required tier and an "Enter test key" button.

## During the Beta
- The Beta starts on the Free tier. Enter the [test key](01-install.md#test-key-unlock-pro-and-cine) to use all Pro and Cine features for free.
- Test keys are updated every week, stay valid for 14 days each, and are published free of charge only on GitHub at AeroCam-Releases.
- When the Beta ends, the app returns to the Free tier once the final key expires.
