# AeroCam User Manual (English)

> Matches **Beta 92.04.08.10** (updated 2026-11-15). Lines marked with 📷 are placeholders for screenshots to be added later.

AeroCam sends photos from your camera straight to your Android phone. You can also control your camera from the phone, edit photos on your phone, and write styles into the camera. **Nikon Z series** cameras are supported for now, with more brands coming later.

AeroCam is an independent third-party app, not affiliated with or authorized by Nikon or any other camera maker.

## Supported Cameras

| Model | Auto receive, camera gallery | Remote shooting | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Z9, Z8, Z6III, Zf, Z5II, Z50II, ZR, and other models with "Connection type" in the menu | ✅ | ✅ | Tested on Zf; reports for other models welcome |
| Z6, Z7, Z6II, Z7II, Z5, Z50, Zfc, Z30, and earlier models | ✅ | ❌ (not supported by these models' connection protocol) | Reports welcome |
| Canon, Sony, Fujifilm | Coming soon | Coming soon | |

If you test AeroCam with another camera model, please let us know what works and what doesn't via [Camera report](https://github.com/AeroCam-Group/AeroCam-Releases/issues/new/choose).

## Requirements
- An Android phone running Android 10 or later.
- A Nikon Z series camera.

## Terms Used in This Manual
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Camera gallery / camera SD card | Photos on the camera's memory card, browsed from your phone over a connection |
| Phone photos / phone gallery | Photos already saved on your phone |
| RAW | Nikon NEF files |
| JPEG.S | Compressed JPEG (JPEG extracted from RAW, JPEG recompressed on your phone, or smaller copy sent over Bluetooth), ending with `_S.JPG` |
| Picture Control | Camera color profiles such as Standard or Flat; custom ones are stored in C1–C9 |
| Pro / Cine | Paid tiers; during Beta, enter the free test key to unlock everything — see [Chapter 9](09-tiers.md) |
