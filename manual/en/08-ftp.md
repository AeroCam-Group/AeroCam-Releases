# 8. FTP Receive (Pro)

Your camera **pushes photos straight to your phone over FTP** right after you take each shot — no need to select photos on the camera or open the gallery. Tested on a Nikon Zf, each ~18 MB RAW file arrives on the phone in about 1 second.

AeroCam has a built-in FTP server, so **you don't need to set up any external server**.

## Best For
- Events, weddings, and sports: photos stream to your phone as you shoot.
- Instant review and social sharing right after taking a shot.

## Requirements
- A camera with built-in FTP upload (Nikon Z f, Z 8, Z 9, Z 6III, etc.).
- Your phone's **mobile hotspot** turned on with the camera connected to it (or both connected to the same router).
- A memory card inserted in the camera (FTP transfers files from the card).

## First-Time Setup
1. Turn on your phone's hotspot.
2. In AeroCam, open the **FTP** card on Home → tap **Start receiving** at the top. The card displays the server details — tap any field to copy it:
   - Server address (e.g., `10.14.29.30`)
   - Port: `2121`
   - User name: `aerocam`
   - Password: 8-character password generated by the app (tap "Regenerate" anytime)
   - Passive mode (PASV): **On**
3. On your camera: **MENU → Network menu → Connect to FTP server → Network settings → Create profile**:
   - Select your phone's hotspot for Wi‑Fi.
   - Choose `FTP` as the server type, enter the address, port, user name, and password above, and enable Passive mode.
4. Activate this FTP connection on the camera. Setup is complete once AeroCam's FTP tile shows "Connected".
5. In the camera's FTP menu, turn on **Auto send** (send after shooting) so every new photo transfers automatically.

Unsure about any step? Tap **ⓘ** next to the FTP tile for a visual guide.

## During Use
- Status indicator: Off, Running · Waiting for camera, Connected, Receiving, or Error.
- The notification shows "FTP receiving · N received" along with the current file name and transfer speed.
- Photos are saved using your **Settings → Receive & notifications** rules (folder, file name, JPEG only, Save JPEG only from RAW, etc.) and logged with source `FTP`.
- If the transfer drops or the camera powers off mid-file, partial files are deleted automatically so broken files never appear in your gallery.
- After turning off the camera, the status updates to disconnected within ~1 minute; turning the camera back on reconnects automatically.
- **Idle auto-stop**: **Settings → Receive & notifications → "FTP idle auto-stop"** (Never, 5, 10, or 30 minutes). Counts from the last received photo, never stops mid-transfer, and notifies you when stopped.

## Things to Keep in Mind
- **Keep your hotspot on.** Some phones turn off their hotspot automatically when no device is using internet data — disable "Turn off hotspot automatically" in your phone's hotspot settings.
- Toggling your hotspot off and on may change your phone's IP address; update it on the camera if needed.
- FTP receive and **Wi‑Fi auto receive cannot run at the same time** — starting one automatically stops the other.
- While FTP is active, the camera is in FTP mode, so **Camera roll and Remote cannot connect over Wi‑Fi**. Use a USB cable instead, or stop FTP receive and switch the camera back to "Connect to PC".
- The FTP server binds only to your hotspot or local Wi‑Fi network and is never exposed to the public internet.
- One-tap connect cannot be used with FTP (One-tap connect starts the camera's own Wi‑Fi, whereas FTP requires the camera to join your phone's network).

## Sending Directly to a NAS
You can also point your camera's FTP settings directly to a home NAS without going through your phone. That is a built-in camera feature and does not require AeroCam.
